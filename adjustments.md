# Summary of Progress: Adjustments to Class

1. The Diagnostic (The Split Classroom)

   * The Problem: The class swung too far into independent workbook reading.
   * The Feedback Split:
     * Visual/Guided Learners: Felt abandoned by raw reading, want visual demos first, and dislike off-topic tangents.
     * Independent/Experienced Learners: Loved the self-paced, solo breakout time to drill concepts.


   * The Goal: Create a hybrid rhythm that satisfies learners who prefer clear visual instruction upfront while preserving independent practice time for self-paced students who want to move fast.

2. The Solution: "Anchor Demos" & Hybrid Flow

   * The Framework: Adopt an "I Do, We Do, You Do" structure for major workbook sections instead of treating the workbook as a standalone self-study manual.
   * Demo Strategy Chosen:
     * Avoid re-teaching every single line of the book or lingering on basics that bore advanced students.
     * Instead, use 1–2 "Anchor Demos" per section (live-coded for syntax/logic or pre-written for larger chunks) to visually kick off the concept before students open the text.


   * The Execution Flow:
     1. Kickoff (10–15 mins): Live-code or walk through an anchor demo so concepts are seen in action.
     2. Bridge: Connect the demo directly to the workbook pages, syntax rules, and reference materials.
     3. Solo/Breakout Time (Remainder): Send students to breakout rooms to read the remaining text and work through exercises at their own individual pace, while the instructor floats and coaches.
